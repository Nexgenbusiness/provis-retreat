Context

What Provis is

Provis Coffee Roasters is a specialty coffee company in North Kansas City. We buy green coffee, roast it, and sell it three ways: direct to people online, by subscription, and wholesale to cafés, churches and universities. From 19 September we also sell it across a counter.

10 AprFirst order shipped
312Customers
10Wholesale accounts
19 SepCafé opens
Where we roastStoneX Specialty Coffee, Riverside. We rent time on their roaster and pay by the pound rather than owning equipment.
Where we sellproviscoffee.com, and 2914 Buchanan in North Kansas City — a two-storey space leased 13 March, with a café downstairs and an events floor upstairs.
How we make moneyA retail bag earns about 41 cents on the dollar. A wholesale pound earns less per pound but moves far more volume. The café opens 19 September and becomes the third.
Where we areFour months of trading. The roastery came within $419 of covering its own costs in July. The café has not opened yet, and it is the largest thing in front of us.

The single biggest fix

Shipping costs us three times what we charge

−$2,494

$1,206 collected against $3,700 of shipping labels. A 33% recovery rate.

349 of 546 orders — 64% — shipped free. Average label $6.78; average collected $2.21.

Even paid shipping loses money. The 197 orders that paid collected $1,206 against $1,290 of labels. Our $5.99 flat rate is under water on its own.

Fix it and contribution goes 32% → 40%, and break-even falls from $7,890 to $6,285 — below every month since May.

September is the crunch

Two launches in one month

19 SepCafé goes live · 2914 Buchanan
+108lb/week of new wholesale
+100lb/week for our own café
106 → 314lb/week, all channels

In one month we nearly triple roasting volume and open a two-storey café. MadeinKC, Greenville, Miss Jojo's, DBU and King's Cross all begin ordering, and the café needs 100 lb a week from day one.

The machine handles it in three roast days and roasting is only 13 hours a week. But it is the month with the least slack in it.

What that means for this room: no new initiatives land in September. Rocks that need Randall or Keegan start in October.

Proposed roast schedule

Two roast days

DayRandallShips
MondayRoast — wholesale and café stockTuesday
TuesdayCafé
WednesdayRoast — subscriptions and web ordersThursday
ThursdayCafé, or overflow roasting above 500 lb/wkFriday
FridaySales — accounts, cuppings, new partners

Order cutoff Friday noon. That gives partners a promise we can keep and ends ad-hoc roasting.

The one decision: move every subscription to a single renewal day. Subscriptions currently renew on all seven days — seven separate pick, pack and ship events. One anchor day collapses that into one batch. It is a settings change and one email, and it is the largest operational win available this year.

Failure rates for this business are genuinely disputed — Square put first-year coffee shop closures near 50%, the National Restaurant Association puts first-year restaurant failure at 14–30%. Take the range rather than the headline. The café risk is still entirely ahead of us. We arrive at that risk with revenue, a customer base and a wholesale book already in hand.
