Where we start

Over breakfast, before any numbers.

"There was no single defining action, no grand program, no one killer innovation."

Jim Collins, Good to Great

Collins found that the companies that made the leap could rarely point to the moment they turned. From outside it looked like a breakthrough. From inside it was one push after another, and nobody noticed the flywheel had started moving.

Eleven months ago this was a domain name and a spreadsheet. There is now a roastery, ten wholesale accounts, 312 customers and a building with our name going on it. Each of us probably turned a different push, and noticed on a different day.

Opening exercise

Two questions. Three minutes to write, then one lap of the table — short answers.

  1. When did this stop feeling like a plan and start feeling like a company? Name the moment.
  2. What is one thing about this business you did not expect to be proud of?
